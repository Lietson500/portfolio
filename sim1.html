Conducting Zone

Trachea (Windpipe)

A wide, hollow tube reinforced with 16–20 C-shaped rings of hyaline cartilage that prevent it from collapsing. It connects the larynx to the primary bronchi, filtering, warming, and humidifying inhaled air.

Primary Function
Air Conduction & Filtration
Key Feature
C-Shaped Cartilage Rings

Did you know? Microscopic hair-like structures called cilia line the trachea and sweep mucus and trapped dust upward at ~1.5 cm per minute.
